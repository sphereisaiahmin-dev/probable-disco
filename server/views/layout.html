about

i am a multidisciplinary artist and creative technologist working across technology, design, and media. i fluently move between the stages of conceptual design, technical development, and live operation. this holistic approach is central to my design ethos: understanding each layer of an experience well enough to bring creative ideas into reality without losing their intention, atmosphere, or cultural meaning.

bringing technical expertise across institutions like: Sphere, Trask House, Lumen & Forge, and Particle Ink. i have created and supported immersive installations, large scale projection, and live productions. i currently work at the Sphere as a Manager of Production Systems. specializing in show control programming, automation, and systems integrations.

i contribute to underground art and music through DJing, event curation, rave production, street art, and disruptive design. my work explores the space between the familiar and the uncanny, drawing from mysticism, Afrofuturism, and religious symbolism. my intention is to challenge the way belief, and technology interact with the contemporary zeitgeist.
